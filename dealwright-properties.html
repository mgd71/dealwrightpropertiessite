Process

How the desk runs

Two lanes feed the same pipeline — one brings supply, one brings capital. Dealwright sits in the middle and makes sure both sides close.

Lane A — Wholesalers & contract holders
01
Submit the deal
Address, contract status, asking assignment fee, any comps you've already pulled.
02
We underwrite it
ARV, comps, and MAO run same-day. If the spread works, it moves forward.
03
JV or buyout
We either co-market it to our buyer list and split the spread, or buy the contract outright at an agreed fee.
04
Close
Title/attorney handles the paperwork. You get paid at closing.
Lane B — Cash buyers
01
Join the list
Tell us your markets, price range, and criteria — rehab, rental, or straight resale.
02
Get the deal ticket
New deals go out with address, ARV, repair estimate, and asking price — no fluff.
03
Review & walk
You or your team verify the numbers. We coordinate access.
04
Close
Assignment or double-close through title/attorney, on your timeline.
